## Context and goal

ADPM is a company in mining and construction. The project called for a digital company profile.

## What we built

I worked on the interface, backend, and deployment using Next.js, Tailwind CSS, and Node.js. The illustration maps three types of information a visitor might explore: company profile, services, and contact. It is an outline of content, not a claim about the original site's page count.

## My role and decisions

My role spanned several parts of the work, from UI/UX to deployment. Working across those steps helped keep design, implementation, and release connected.

## Results and impact

The company website was built and deployed. The project notes do not record traffic, inquiries, or post-launch business results, so I do not claim a measured outcome there.

## Trade-offs and questions to measure

A profile site needs enough detail to establish context, while making services and contact easy to find. A next iteration could measure page performance, how easily content can be updated, and inquiries that start on the site.

## What I learned

Handling design and deployment together reminds me to look at the experience from first visit through real use. The initial visual only helps when important information is easy to read and maintain.
