## Context and goal

While handling land program administration, I managed official travel documents. The main need was to standardize data entry and shorten the SPJ reporting cycle for staff across the directorate.

## What we built

I created dynamic Excel VBA input forms and automated report templates. The form helped staff enter information consistently, and the template produced reports in a standard format.

## My role and decisions

I mapped the required inputs, built the forms and automation, then adjusted the templates for about 50 directorate staff. Excel fit the tools the team was already using.

## Results and impact

Available project notes report processing time falling from around 20 minutes to under 5 minutes per report cycle, alongside a reported reduction of more than 60 percent in manual errors.

The measurement method and comparison period are not specified. I keep these figures as reported results without extending them into a broader productivity claim.

## Trade-offs and questions to measure

Excel automation can fit a familiar workflow, but Office version compatibility and file management need attention so everyone uses the same template. For a growing process, processing time, corrections, and macro maintenance would be useful to track together.

## What I learned

Automation is valuable when it removes a repeated step that people feel every day. Starting with the form and consistent validation made reporting more uniform without asking the team to replace its existing tools at once.
