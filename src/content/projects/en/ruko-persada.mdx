## Context and goal

Ruko Persada Regency needed a digital presence to show its properties. A listing website helps prospective buyers browse homes and contact the developer.

## What we built

I built a responsive interface for property listings and added contact and inquiry features. The implementation used React, Tailwind CSS, and Node.js.

## My role and decisions

The implementation focused on keeping the listings readable at different screen sizes and placing a way to ask about a property near its details.

## Results and impact

The recorded result is a responsive listing website with contact and inquiry features. The project notes do not include listing counts, inquiries, or transactions that came from the site.

## Trade-offs and questions to measure

Listings give people an initial view, but their usefulness depends on keeping property details current. A next iteration could track how quickly the team updates availability and whether visitors complete the inquiry path.

## What I learned

A property page needs to connect information with an action. Both the clarity of a listing card and the route to contact matter to prospective buyers.
