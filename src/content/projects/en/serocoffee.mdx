## Context and goal

Serocoffee is a landing page for a coffee brand. The page introduces the brand and gives visitors a route to continue their interaction.

Project notes do not include brand research or final visual assets, so this illustration describes the page's information flow rather than the original site's appearance.

## What we built

I built the landing page with Astro and Hono, then added interface animation using GSAP. The illustration places the first impression, product story, and next action in one reading path.

## My role and decisions

I handled website implementation and motion. GSAP gave me control over movement between elements, while each animation still needed to support the information on screen.

## Results and impact

The landing page and interface animation were delivered. Project notes do not record traffic, conversion, or brand outcomes after release.

## Trade-offs and questions to measure

Motion can give a page character, but animations that hold content back can frustrate people who came for information. In another iteration I would check duration, touch behavior, and mobile performance.

## What I learned

Animation is most useful when it directs attention to an important part of the page. A sequence should read as one story, not a collection of unrelated effects.
